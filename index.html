Example: Enter 8/250 in the Hissa box. Spaces around the slash are also accepted. The person receives 8 ÷ 250 of the total land, equivalent to 3.20%.

Calculation rules
1 Kanal = 180 Sarsai; 1 Marla = 9 Sarsai.
Individual owned area = Total Sarsai × (Hissa numerator ÷ Hissa denominator).
Unutilised land = Total land − Sum of all individual owned areas. Ownership percentages are calculated from each entered fraction.
